Fixed fitted probes; no refit and no sentence resampling. Per-statement results descriptive; pooled AUC/accuracy and ΔM=no_mlp−clean secondary. Intervals unadjusted. A dataset satisfies the preregistered-style frozen conjunction criterion only if BOTH primary95%interval lower bounds exceed0. 'Replicates on both datasets' requires that criterion in BOTH datasets; do not select the favorable one or combine their samples to rescue significance.

If primary contrasts fail, are uncertain, or differ between datasets, report that limitation and stop. Positive block6 results do not establish final-state/native-LM improvement, lexical-semantic suppression, information erasure, or cross-architecture universality. Different corpus baselines, intensity schemes, token templates and model components bound comparison of raw effect magnitudes. These datasets have already been used in this research; independence is of the newly tested model outcomes from window selection, not a claim that all test data or pretraining histories are unseen.
