# Full-depth matched audio causal validation

This is a mechanism validation of the previously selected post-block 17 leverage point, not a new trained model. Frozen clean prefixes use the same prompt and single-token verbalizers as the prior direct-edge experiments (happy 6247; sad 12421). All 96 strict happy/sad pairs are intervened in both directions at every post-block boundary 0–23. The donor is the opposite emotion with actor, statement, repetition, and intensity fixed. Only the 300 audio residual positions are replaced; the target prompt and decision residual at the intervention boundary remain unchanged.

For each layer, the pair score averages the two donor-aligned changes: `[S(sad←happy)−S(sad) + S(happy)−S(happy←sad)]/2`. Four pair scores are averaged per actor. We resample the 24 actors 10000 times with replacement using NumPy seed 20260915, maintaining all within-actor repetitions, statements, layers, and intervention directions together. The pointwise interval uses bootstrap percentiles. A simultaneous band additionally uses the 95 th percentile of the maximum absolute centered bootstrap deviation across all 24 layers (unstudentized). Paired layer-minus 17 differences use the same actor draws, with their own 24-layer simultaneous band. These are approximate cluster-bootstrap intervals, not exact finite-sample guarantees.

The global point-estimate peak alone does not establish a uniquely privileged layer. Assess the full curve and simultaneous layer-minus 17 contrasts; a nonsignificant difference does not prove equivalence. These interventions replace complete matched audio residual states and can include non-emotion acoustic differences. They do not by themselves establish an emotion-specific direction.

Numerical gates: no-op self replacement at all 24 layers for both samples of the first pair; clean single-token sequence-likelihood parity for all 192 samples; patched sequence-likelihood parity at all 24 layers for the first pair; exact post 23 structural zero for every sample (there is no later block routing audio to the untouched decision position); historical clean and post 17 donor margins matched by sample ID. The final-layer zero is an architectural boundary check, not evidence that the final audio states contain no emotion.
